03decision engine

Deterministic by design

Four pure steps from facts to a recommended action. Same input, same decision — no randomness. A control group is held out so uplift is measurable.

STEP 1

Signals

facts with recorded evidence per customer — contracts, tickets, consent

STEP 2

Scoring

STEP 3

Arbitration

policy weights · min ΔCLV · blocked lists

STEP 4

Baseline NBA

action · product · channel — control group held out for measurement

same input → same decision · no randomness reviews & overrides are separate records — nothing is rewritten
04AI layer

AI on top — never in charge

The model receives an evidence-linked policy packet and must answer in strict JSON. Validation checks every quote and number; bounded repair allows one draft and two fixes — then it stops.
